## Positioning

The market is supplying models, skills, computer control, sandboxes, persistent
agents, A2A, and generation. The scarce layer is organizational:

What is this organization trying to accomplish? What does it know? Which agent
should act? What may it access? What may it change? What counts as good? What
happened last time? What should happen next?

Execution agents are becoming abundant. Governed organizational intelligence is not.
